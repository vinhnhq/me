Bảo Minh Gia Định is an insurance company. I built its sales portal as a freelancer and ran it in production: about 50 sales staff used it to sell insurance contracts, and their customers used it to manage them.

## How it was built

It used the same stack as [K-Studio](/blog/k-studio), but with Cursor as an assistant, not an agent: I wrote much more of the code by hand. Comparing the two projects is what convinced me how far a good agent harness goes.

## How it ended

The portal ran until April 2026, when the parent company moved its subsidiaries onto a group-wide platform and the portal was retired.
